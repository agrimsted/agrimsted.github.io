While working at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory I contributed to the federal COVID-19 response as part of the COVID-19 Operations Team (often 20+ collaborators) from August 2021 to February 2023. 
My role involved developing automated data analytics pipelines that integrated numerous streams of public health data into quality-controlled reports. 
These reports were released routinely and publicly disseminated via healthdata.gov. Below are previews and links to example final releases from February 2023.

## State Profile Report (Example: Maryland)

This weekly series offered state- and county-level indicators on COVID-19 trends, including recent changes over the prior month.

## Community Profile Report

This series (initially daily, then weekly) provided national and regional overviews of COVID-19 impacts, focusing on community-level metrics like transmission rates and healthcare strain.
