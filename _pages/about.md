I am a third-year PhD student in Industrial & Operations Engineering (IOE) at the University of Michigan, with a concentration in Operations Research. I am advised by [Professor Brian Denton](<https://btdenton.engin.umich.edu>). My research interests are broadly in sequential decision-making and optimization under uncertainty. In particular, I develop data-driven optimization and reinforcement learning methods for sequential decision-making, with applications in healthcare and public policy.

Before graduate school, I worked as an Operations Research Analyst in the Decision Systems Group at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory for three years. I received my Bachelor of Science in Operations Research from Cornell University in May 2021.
